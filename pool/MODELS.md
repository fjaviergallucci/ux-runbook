# Models and effort

Reviewed: 2026-11-14

Each seat is a fresh sub-agent, and a sub-agent can be started on a chosen model at a chosen reasoning
effort. So every card carries a suggested model and a suggested effort. The cards hold the values. This
file holds the reasons, and the day they were last reviewed, because which model suits what goes stale
as fast as the list of tools.

**They are suggestions, never instructions, and nothing in the kit enforces them.** Cast shows them on the
cast sheet, and the founder may change any of them. A seat with no suggestion runs on the session's
model.

The aim is the best tool for the job, not the cheapest. The models are tiers of capability, not
specialists: no model is "the one for words" or "the one for layout". A card names a model by its
family, never by a version.

## What the suggestions rest on

1. **Judgement or mechanics.** When the founder picks from what a seat makes, or when the seat's verdict
   opens or closes a stage, it is suggested on the most capable model. When its work is tightly
   specified, with little to decide, it is suggested on a faster one.
2. **A different model for the judge.** Where another model is at hand, a judge or an audience is
   suggested on a different model from the maker whose work it reads. Two models share fewer blind spots
   than two copies of one.
3. **Range where ideas are born.** When several describers sit at Structure, they are suggested across
   different models. A card holds one value, so the describer's card names one model, and Cast spreads
   the instances over what is available. An outside describer adds a mind from another maker altogether.
4. **Effort is the second dial.** Higher for a seat that makes ideas or judges them. Lower for a
   mechanical one, where more thought changes nothing.

The table assumes this order of capability, the most capable first: `fable`, `opus`, `sonnet`, `haiku`.
When that order changes, the table changes with it. The four reasons do not.

## The seats

| Seat | Suggested model | Suggested effort | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `maker.structure-describer` | `fable` | `high` | It makes the ideas the founder picks from. With several describers, see reason 3. |
| `maker.outside-describer` | `none` | `none` | It runs on its engine: the outside model the project's profile names. |
| `maker.screen-generator` | `none` | `none` | It runs on its engine. Without the tool, the maker that stands in runs on the session's model. |
| `maker.transcriber` | `sonnet` | `medium` | Copying to a strict format, with nothing to invent. |
| `maker.interaction-designer` | `fable` | `high` | Judgement: every state and edge the build will depend on. |

| `maker.prototype-builder` | `opus` | `medium` | Careful building to a specification that is already decided. |

| `judge.truth` | `opus` | `high` | Reason 2. Careful tracing, where one missed row misleads her. |
| `judge.words` | `opus` | `high` | Reason 2. An ear for each language, on lines another model wrote. |
| `judge.accessibility` | `opus` | `high` | Reason 2. Judgement and measurement together. |
