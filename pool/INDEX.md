# The pool, on one page

Cast reads this page to choose seats, not the cards, and then the checks table of each judge it seats.
It holds every seat the kit ships, in one row, and every person who can be seated. A row's `job` and
`cast-when` are its card's own, word for word, and a test holds the two together. The model and the
effort each seat is suggested on are in `pool/MODELS.md`. A project's own cards and people are in its
own `pool/`, not here: Cast reads both.

| id | job | cast-when |
|---|---|---|
| `maker.structure-describer` | Propose one way to solve it, as a walkthrough. | The design has a structure to choose, which is every design from a surface up. |
| `maker.outside-describer` | Propose one way to solve it, from a mind outside the session's own. | The project has turned an outside model on, and the design is worth one more kind of mind. |
| `maker.screen-generator` | Generate the one or two screens that carry the idea, knowing nothing of the design system. | A fresh pass is cast, or the founder wants one more kind of proposal at Structure. |
| `maker.transcriber` | Write down the structure of a screen someone else made, adding nothing. | A proposal arrived as a screen, or as prose, and must join the board in the walkthrough format. |
| `maker.interaction-designer` | Every state, every rule and every edge case of the chosen structure. | A structure is chosen, or a change touches how something behaves. |
| `maker.interface-writer` | Every string the interface shows, written for each language. | The design shows words, which is every design, or a change touches what something says. |
| `maker.prototype-builder` | Build the clickable plain prototype, every state in every language, on the shared kit. | The design has more than one state or more than one language to see before anyone builds. |
| `maker.visual-designer` | The finished screens, on the project's own tokens. | The detail is approved and the screens need their final look, or a change is visual. |
| `judge.usability` | Can she finish the job without help? | The design gives her something to do, which is every design from an adjustment up. |
| `judge.truth` | Does everything shown exist in the product? | The design shows data or offers actions the product must really have, and always when it is a public page. |
| `judge.words` | Does it sound like a person, and say the same in each language? | The design shows words she has to read in order to act, in one language or several. |
| `judge.accessibility` | Can someone who does not see, point or read as the designer does still use it? | The design has controls to operate or content to read, which is every design from a surface up. |
| `judge.visual-craft` | Is it good of its kind? | The screens have their final look, or today's interface is read before it is redesigned. |
| `judge.system-fidelity` | Is it made of this product's parts? | The project has a design system, and the screens are drawn on it or built from it. |
| `judge.profile` | Would this work for this profession? | The experience is for one profession or several, one instance for each profile it serves; or for everyone, when two contrasting profiles sit, the least comfortable with technology and the one with the highest stakes. |
| `judge.cold-implementer` | Could I build this without asking? | A handoff folder has been written, which is every design that will be built. |
| `audience.task-reader` | Try the task, cold, as one person, and say where I got stuck. | The design gives her a task to try, from a surface up; one instance sits as the primary person, and one as a contrast person the makers were not briefed on. |

## People

The kit's five people have no profession. They serve a project that has no profiles yet, and they serve
as contrast seats: an audience seated as someone the makers were not briefed on, because a design made
to a card is always understood by that card. Each is an archetype and a guess, set in the kit's example
product. A project's own people, each of a profile, are in its own `pool/persons/`.

| id | the moment |
|---|---|
| `person.first-timer` | Her first morning at the front desk, nobody to ask, the screen open for the first time and a man waiting. |
| `person.daily-expert` | Three years on this screen, the phone at her ear, her hand already where the button was yesterday. |
| `person.hurried-on-a-phone` | Walking a corridor with her arms full, the phone in one hand, fifteen seconds, about to be interrupted. |
| `person.keyboard-user` | Alone at the desk after closing, the mouse in a drawer, fourteen bookings to move by keyboard before she can go home. |
| `person.second-language-reader` | A quiet half hour, nobody watching, and a message in her second language that she must be sure of before she acts and replies. |
