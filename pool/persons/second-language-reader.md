# The second-language reader

An archetype, not someone anyone met: every line on this card is a guess.
She stands for whoever reads an interface in a language that is not her first. Her scene is set in the
kit's example product, a room-booking tool at a small clinic's front desk: in another product, keep her
and change the setting.

## The moment

Linh has worked the front desk of a small clinic for two months. The language on the screen is her
second. She speaks it with patients all day without trouble. Reading it is slower, word by word, and she
reads every word, because skimming is the one thing she cannot yet do in it. It is the middle of the
afternoon, and she has just tried to book a room in the room-booking tool. Where she expected the booking
to appear, a message has appeared, with two buttons under it. A patient is waiting at the desk. Her
phone, with a translation app on it, is face down beside the keyboard, and she would rather not pick it
up in front of him.

## The job

Understand what the screen has just told her, whether the booking went through and what it wants from her
now, well enough to press the right one of the two buttons, without a colleague and without the phone.
Done, to her, is knowing the room is booked, not hoping so.

## What she never does

- Skim. She reads every word in order, so every extra word costs her, and a long sentence loses her
  before its verb.
- Guess at an idiom, a joke, a clipped phrase or an abbreviation: in English, "You're all set", "Heads
  up", "N/A". She takes each word at its dictionary meaning, and stops when that makes no sense.
- Take two words for one thing. If the button says "Book" and the message says "Reserve", she looks for
  the difference between them.
- Press a button she is unsure of when she cannot tell whether it can be undone.
- Take anything from tone. A friendly aside or an exclamation mark is more words to get through.
- Ask, with a patient standing there.

## The check

Read word by word, each word at its dictionary meaning, would this screen tell her what has happened and
which button to press?
