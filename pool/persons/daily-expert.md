# The daily expert

An archetype, not someone anyone met: every line on this card is a guess.
She stands for whoever uses a product all day and no longer reads it, and for what a change costs her.
Her scene is set in the kit's example product, a room-booking tool at a small clinic's front desk: in
another product, keep her and change the setting.

## The moment

Marta has run the front desk of a small clinic for three years, and the room-booking tool has been open
on her screen for all of them. It is twenty to ten, the busiest quarter of an hour of her morning. The
phone is wedged between her shoulder and her ear, a caller wants tomorrow's appointment moved, and her
right hand is already travelling to where the button has always been. She stopped reading this screen
long ago: she knows where things are. Overnight the screen was changed, and nobody told her.

## The job

Move the caller's booking to another room and another time before the caller has finished explaining, as
she has done forty times a day for three years, in the number of presses it took yesterday. Done, to her,
is saying "that's moved" without having looked at the screen for longer than a glance.

## What she never does

- Read the label on something she has pressed a thousand times. She goes by place and by shape, so a
  control that has moved is, to her, a control that has gone.
- Read what is new: a banner, a tip, a dot on a menu. She dismisses it unread, and will not find the new
  thing by it.
- Wait for the screen. She presses ahead of it and types before a dialog has finished opening, so
  whatever appears under her hand at that moment is pressed.
- Take an added step in silence. A confirmation she did not need yesterday is counted forty times a day,
  and resented each time.
- Walk through steps laid out for a beginner, or keep undoing a choice the screen made for her.
- Look for a faster way that nobody showed her. The way she knows is the way she uses.

## The check

Does what she does forty times a day still take the same presses in the same places as yesterday, and if
not, what does she get for the difference?
