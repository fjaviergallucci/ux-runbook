# The keyboard user

An archetype, not someone anyone met: every line on this card is a guess.
She stands for whoever works a screen without a pointer, whatever the reason. Her scene is set in the
kit's example product, a room-booking tool at a small clinic's front desk: in another product, keep her
and change the setting.

## The moment

It is ten past six, the clinic is closed, and Linh is alone at the front desk with half the lights off.
The floor of the gym is being relaid tomorrow, so the fourteen bookings in it have to move to other rooms
before she can go home. Both hands are on the keyboard, where they have been all day: a tendon in her
right wrist flares when she grips a mouse, so a year ago she stopped, and the mouse is in a drawer. She
moves through the room-booking tool with Tab, the arrow keys, Enter and Escape, as fast as she types,
which is fast. She has just moved the first booking and pressed Tab for the second, and is looking for
the one thing she needs before she presses anything else: the mark that shows where on the screen she is.

## The job

Move fourteen bookings to other rooms, one after another, without the pointer: reach a booking, open it,
choose a room, confirm, and land on the next booking, not back at the top of the list. Done, to her, is
the last one moved, none moved twice and none skipped, at the speed of her hands and not of the screen.

## What she never does

- Touch the pointer. What shows only on hover, opens only on a click or moves only by dragging is not
  there for her.
- Press Enter when she cannot see what has the focus. She stops, and looks for it.
- Put up with thirty presses of Tab for each booking. After an evening of it she asks for the paper diary
  back.
- Stay in a dialog that Escape does not close, or trust one that drops her at the top of the page when it
  closes.
- Guess a shortcut. She uses the ones the screen shows her.
- Follow an order that jumps about. She expects Tab to go where the eye would go next.

## The check

Can she always see where she is, reach the next thing she needs in a few keys and get back, without once
touching the pointer?
