# Detail's other forms: an adjustment, a flow

The skill gives Detail as it runs in full, for one surface. Two other forms keep every rule of it and
change only how much is done. A correction is a third, and has a page of its own:
`references/correction.md`.

## Routed `short`: an adjustment

Detail is what changes, and no more.

- **Only the seats of the cast sheet's "Detail" table run,** on the screens, the states and the
  strings the change touches. A maker rewrites those rows and those keys alone, and a reader reads
  those pages.
- **Every other heading of `DETAIL.md` says "Unchanged by this adjustment".**
- **A stage the route skips is left out of `built-from` and of the carry list,** and so is the cast
  sheet's table for it: the sheet has none. Where it skips Structure, the stage before this one is
  Cast: you read `<package>/03-cast.md`, and what its carry list names.
- **With Structure skipped there is no chosen proposal, and no hand-over names `<chosen>`.** A seat
  takes the structure from what it is handed besides: the states and the rules, and the context's
  "What exists today" where its card reads the context. The "Do not open" lists stand as they are.
- **With no prototype builder cast, no page is generated and none is photographed.** Step 5 of
  the skill is not run, the readers are handed the changed rows and strings, and the decision page
  gives each changed row and string as its option's line. The carry list then has no line for the
  prototype's folder or the pictures' folder: neither exists.
- **The fix round, the re-read and the gate are the skill's,** unchanged.

## Sized `flow`: surface by surface

A flow is several surfaces that form one task. Structure treated it as one thing. Detail goes
surface by surface, in the order the chosen structure lists them.

- **What repeats for each surface:** steps 3 to 8 of the skill. The three makers in their order,
  the pictures, the readers, the one fix round and the one re-read. A surface is finished before
  the next one begins, and each has its one fix round.
- **What every surface writes into, and is one for the flow:** `states.md`, `strings.json`, so that
  one word stays one thing across the surfaces, and `prototype/` with its one index. In `DETAIL.md`,
  a `### <surface>` under each heading that differs from one surface to the next.
- **A reader's file takes the surface's name** after its seat, as
  `<package>/05-detail/reads/<seat>-<surface>.md`.
- **What is done once, for the whole flow:** the questions of step 4 that touch more than one
  surface; the words' page, where the kind asks for one; the decision page, with the pictures of
  each surface among its option's and the open findings of all of them; the gate; the carry list;
  the stamp. He approves the flow, not a surface.
