# The seat at Handoff: what it is handed, and what it does not open

One seat of the kit sits at Handoff: the cold implementer. Its brief hands it what its card's `reads`
allows and this page lists, by its absolute path, and lists under "Do not open" what its card's
`never-reads` names, as this package holds it. Nothing is pasted into the brief. How a seat is
started, on which model, and the rules of a shared machine are in
`skills/design/references/loop.md`, under "Starting a seat". The brief is written into
`<package>/work/`, and keeps the template's last line: a seat that starts a browser stops only the
one it started.

A card of the project's own that the cast sheet's "Handoff" table seats is handed what its header's
`reads` allows, by the same rule, and of the package no more than the folder.

No maker sits here. You wrote the folder, so you are the one who answers what the seat finds in it,
and you never read it for the gate yourself: the seat that tests the folder is one that was not here.

## The reader

### `judge.cold-implementer`

- **Its brief,** from `templates/briefs/judge.md`: what it judges is the handoff folder, as the
  builder will get it, with the widths, the themes and the languages its checks name. "Checks" reads
  "none" on both lines: the seat has no optional check. The brief says nothing of how the design
  came to be, of what you expect it to find, or of what was hard to write.
- **Handed:** `<package>/07-handoff/`, the handoff folder, whole, by its absolute path, and nothing
  else. `HANDOFF.md` in it is where it starts.
- **Do not open:** everything else in the package. By name: `<package>/work/`, where your notes and
  this brief's drafts are; `<package>/04-structure/MAP.sealed.json`, the author map, and
  `<package>/04-structure/CHOSEN.md`, which names who made which letter; every reads folder, and
  `<package>/07-handoff-gate/`, which holds a ledger of this seat on a re-read; `<package>/03-cast.md`;
  every other stage's folder, from `<package>/00-frame.md` to `<package>/06-look/`;
  `<package>/DECISIONS.md`; `<package>/build/`; and the component source, with the rest of the
  project's own source: where the folder sends it there, it lists the reference and does not follow.
- **Writes:** its ledger, `<package>/07-handoff-gate/judge.cold-implementer.md`, in the shape of
  `templates/outputs/LEDGER.md`: beside the folder, never in it, so that the folder a builder gets
  holds no ledger and a re-read finds none there.
- **Its reply** is the ledger's path, its verdict, in the seat's three words (*ready*, *ready with
  defaults*, *not ready*), and the count of questions and of guesses.
- **It drives no browser.**

## Closing its items

Every item of the ledger is closed in the folder, in the file its fix names, one of three ways.
Nothing is answered in your reply to the founder, in the chat or in the ledger: a builder gets none
of those.

| The item | What you do |
|---|---|
| **The answer is in what the carry list hands you,** and the folder left it out or said it twice. | Write it in the folder, word for word where it is hers to read. Two files that disagree are made to agree with what was approved. |
| **It is how something is built:** where a file goes, how state is held. | Write it under "Left to the builder" of `HANDOFF.md`: it is the builder's, and now says so. |
| **Nobody decided it.** | Put it to the founder, one question at a time, as step 9 of the skill says. His answer is written into the folder as he said it. Where he leaves it open, write a default there, marked "Default:", with the line "nobody decided this: build it so, or write another way down as a deviation". |

Three items come up on every folder, and each is closed the first way:

- **A reference that points outside the folder.** Write out what it pointed to, and take the
  reference out. A path into the project's source stays, once you have opened it and seen that it
  is there.
- **`../build/`.** It is the one way out of the folder, and it is meant: `HANDOFF.md` names it,
  under "What you write back", as where the build writes its three pages. See that the sentence
  says so plainly, and close the item with it. It is never taken out.
- **A state with no page, or a string with no key.** A miss in the folder's own lists is yours to
  put right when the approved design holds it. When the approved design itself lacks it, it is not
  Handoff's: it goes to the founder, and the stage it belongs to is his to reopen.

And three things hold while you close them:

- **A default is his to see.** Each one is listed on the decision page under `findings`, so that his
  go covers it. You propose a default in one line and never settle one in silence.
- **A page or a check that changed is proved again,** by the commands of steps 3 and 6 of the
  skill, before the re-read.
- **Its row waits for its last read.** The seat, what it read, its four core checks, the model it
  ran on and its verdict, with each of its items under "Findings" and what became of it (answered
  in the folder, left to the builder, or a default), go into `COVERAGE.md` once it will not read
  again: after the re-read when an item blocked, and otherwise once its items are closed. A re-read
  that found its own first findings in the folder would not be reading cold.

## The re-read

Only when an item was marked *blocks*. One fresh sub-agent in the same seat, with the first read's
brief, which says it is a re-read. It names one file more: the earlier ledger,
`<package>/07-handoff-gate/judge.cold-implementer.md`, to be opened only after it has read the folder
cold and written its lists. It marks each earlier item *fixed*, *partly* or *still present*, in
`<package>/07-handoff-gate/judge.cold-implementer-reread.md`, beside the first and never over it.

There is no second round. What is still present goes to the founder with the gate, as an open
finding. The handoff is ready when the lists are empty, or every item left has its answer or its
default written in the folder.
