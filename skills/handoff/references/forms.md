# Handoff's other forms: an adjustment, a flow, a correction

The skill gives Handoff as it runs in full, for one surface. Three other forms keep every rule of it
and change only how much is done.

## Routed `short`: an adjustment

Handoff is what changes, and no more.

- **The folder holds only what the change touches:** the screens and the states that were redrawn,
  their pictures, the strings that changed, and the checks that hold them. `SPEC.md`, `PARTS.md` and
  `ACCEPTANCE.md` have rows for those alone, and every other heading says "Unchanged by this
  adjustment".
- **`HANDOFF.md` says what stays as it is,** under "Must not change": the builder touches nothing
  the adjustment did not.
- **A stage the route skips is left out of `built-from`,** and its sections are on no line of the
  carry list: nothing is written from it.
- **With Structure skipped there is no chosen structure.** "Regions, in priority order" reads
  "Unchanged by this adjustment", unless the change moves an element: then it gives the regions as
  the changed screens draw them.
- **The cold implementer sits when the cast sheet's "Handoff" table seats it,** and reads the folder
  as the skill says. Steps 6, 9 and 10 and the gate are the skill's, unchanged.

## Sized `flow`: one folder, surface by surface

A flow is several surfaces that form one task. It is handed off once, as one folder.

- **`SPEC.md` repeats its block for each surface,** in the order Detail went.
- **`prototype/` holds the pages of every surface,** with one index, grouped by surface first.
- **A check names its `screen`** wherever two surfaces carry the same id.
- **The slices of `HANDOFF.md` follow the task:** in the order she meets the surfaces, unless one
  must exist before another can be built. A slice may cross two surfaces when a state of one cannot
  be reached without the other.
- **A ledger of the flow's readers takes its surface's name,** as Detail and Look wrote it: each is a
  row of `COVERAGE.md`.
- **One cold implementer, one decision page, one go, one stamp.**

## Sized `correction`: one paragraph, and no seat

A correction is one wrong thing put right: a label, a token or a spacing value. Detail showed it
before and after, and he nodded. What a correction is, and when it stops being one, is the
conductor's: `skills/design/references/routes.md`, under "A correction".

- **No seat is cast,** so no cold implementer reads, and no brief is written. Steps 3 to 10 of the
  skill are not run, and no folder is filled.
- **Begin,** as step 1 of the skill says. Copy the template to `<package>/07-handoff/HANDOFF.md`, and
  write `built-from` as the route gives it: `[detail@<version>, frame@<version>]`, each at the
  version `status` prints. You read the detail, `<package>/05-detail/DETAIL.md`, and what its carry
  list names: the two pictures.
- **Write one paragraph, under "Task":** the one thing changed, as "is → becomes", where it is in the
  product, by its screen, its element and the file that holds it, and the picture that shows it done:
  the one after. "Done when" is that picture. Every other heading says "Not needed for this
  correction", and "What you write back" says that the session that conducts applies it.
- **His nod is the go.** A correction costs one nod, and he gave it at Detail. Write under
  `## Reviewed by the founder` that the go is his nod at Detail, with its words, copied as `DETAIL.md`
  has them, and stamp with those words, by the `stamp` of the skill's gate. Nothing is asked again,
  and no decision page is built.
- **No script checks a correction.** No `acceptance.json` is written, and no command of the skill
  is run but those of the state: a correction has no page of a prototype for a check to open. Its
  record is the picture of before and after, and the one-paragraph record Detail wrote. "Verify"
  says so: the build is held to the picture after, by eye.
- **After the stamp, the session that conducts applies the change,** as the paragraph says, and
  nothing else. That is the build. The same session then accepts it, in Acceptance's short form:
  a correction is the one design where the session that built also accepts.
- **If it turns out to touch more** than a label, a token or a spacing value, stop, say so to the
  founder, and resize, as the conductor's "Resuming" says.
