# The fresh pass

What exists is itself a bias: the design system, today's screens, the product's habits. A maker given
none of it returns concepts nobody inside the product would have proposed. So a design can be made
twice, once inside the system and once with no knowledge of it, and the two compared.

## Offered, not automatic

- Cast states whether a fresh pass runs at Structure, at Look, or at both, with which makers and why:
  the cast sheet has a section for it (`templates/package/03-cast.md`).
- The founder can ask for one at any decision page.
- It does not run at Detail, where the work is to complete a chosen structure, not to explore.
- You never add one yourself. It costs agents, and it is his to ask for.

## What a fresh maker is given, and what it is not

Its brief is the fresh brief, written from `templates/package/04-structure/BRIEF-fresh.md`. The template
holds two forms, one for Structure and one for Look: keep the one of the stage, as its first lines say.
The founder reads the brief before it goes out, like any brief.

- **Given:** the person and her moment, the job, the tasks, the language, the device, and what the
  product can show and do, so that nothing is invented.
- **Not given:** the design system, the shared kit, today's screens and navigation, the conventions of
  the sibling surfaces.

"No knowledge" means no knowledge of form, not of what the product does. If anything held back reaches a
fresh maker, in its brief or in a file it was handed, that maker's work is no longer fresh: say so, and
start the seat again, clean.

## Who makes it, and how much

- Engines that return a whole concept, structure and look together, as a picture: a screen generator, an
  outside model, a Claude maker with a taste skill as its lens. The cast names them, and an engine the
  project has not turned on is never used.
- The one or two screens that carry the idea, never the whole flow.

## At Structure it is shown twice

1. **On the board.** The structure of each fresh concept is written into the walkthrough format, drawn
   in the shared kit, and joins the blind board as one more letter. Structures are compared like with
   like, and the founder does not know which came from where. **The pick is made on the board.**
2. **In a gallery beneath the board.** The same concepts as they came, labelled as fresh, and never
   mixed with the lettered options. In the gallery he marks what he likes, and his marks are carried to
   Look.

## At Look it is shown beside the system version

On the same screen, with the same content. The profile judge and one audience rank the two. The truth
judge flags anything the product cannot do. The system fidelity judge lists what the system lacks to
make each thing he may want.

## Three outcomes, and only three

1. **Nothing to borrow.** The system version stands. The gallery stays in the record.
2. **Borrow named things.** Each is rebuilt on the project's tokens by the visual designer. Anything the
   system lacks enters as a named extension of the system (a new token, part or pattern), listed in the
   handoff's `PARTS.md` with what it costs.
3. **The fresh concept wins as a whole.** That is a change of direction for this surface, and its cost
   is on the decision page before he chooses.

There is no fourth: no "a bit of both" without names, and no fresh screen that reaches the build by
itself. **Never is a fresh screen handed off as it came.**
