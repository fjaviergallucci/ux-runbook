# Lessons

Every design teaches something: a seat that caught what the others missed, a brief that gave the answer
away, a model that suited a job. Two files, both appended, never rewritten, keep it. An entry that
proved wrong is answered by a later one.

## The project's lessons

`LESSONS.md`, in the folder that holds the project's packages, beside its profile. Setup writes it from
`templates/project/LESSONS.md`. After every design, append one entry in the shape that template gives.
Its heading is the day, the design's name, and its kind and size:

`## 2026-11-14 · today · working-surface, surface`

- **What was tried.** The route that ran: the stages, the makers, any fresh pass.
- **What happened.** Numbered, one thing each: what was expected, and what came.
- **What the next design does differently.** One line for each thing above that calls for one.
- **The founder's words.** Where they mattered, as he said them.
- **Cost.** The agents each stage started, and the tokens, as a tool counted them.
- **Seats whose findings changed a decision.** One row for each, with the model it ran on:

| Seat | Model it ran on | The finding | The decision it changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| `judge.accessibility` | `opus` | At 390 px the hours of the day scroll sideways, and the line at now leaves the screen. | The grid of the day was not picked. |

That last line is the one that matters most. It records which seats' findings changed a decision, and the
model each ran on, and it is what prunes the pool and corrects the model suggestions: a seat is kept or
dropped on this record. Where an outside model or a fresh maker sat, say whether what it made was picked.

Write the entry from the package (its decisions, its ledgers, its cast sheet, its state), never from
what you remember of the chat. A number you name is counted by a tool first.

## The kit's lessons

The kit keeps a `LESSONS.md` of its own, at its root, for only what is true of the process on any
project: nothing of one product, one person or one profession. You do not write to it. When a design
taught something that would hold anywhere, propose the entry to the founder, in the same shape. It lands
in the kit's repository by an ordinary change, made there.

## When

When a design ends: after Acceptance, or when it is stopped for good. A design that stops early teaches
too: say what stopped it.
