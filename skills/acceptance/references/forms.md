# Acceptance's other forms: an adjustment, a flow, a correction, a fix that comes back

The skill gives Acceptance as it runs in full, for one surface, the first time a build is read. Four
other forms keep every rule of it and change only how much is done.

## Routed `short`: an adjustment

Acceptance is what changed, and no more.

- **Only the states the handoff folder holds are driven:** those the adjustment touched. Their
  checks are run, and their pictures taken.
- **Only the seats of the cast sheet's "Acceptance" table read.** With no audience on it, nobody
  reads as her, and the report says so.
- **One more thing is looked at: what was not meant to change.** For each screen the adjustment
  touched, photograph its ordinary state and put the picture beside the approved one. A difference
  outside the change is an item of the report: *deviates*.
- **Every other heading of `REPORT.md` says "Unchanged by this adjustment".** The gate is the
  skill's, unchanged.

## Sized `flow`: every surface, accepted once

A flow is several surfaces that form one task. It was handed off as one folder, and it is accepted
as one build.

- **Every state of every surface is driven,** in the order of `ACCEPTANCE.md`. Where two surfaces
  have a state of the same name, its results file takes the screen's name first, as
  `references/driving.md` says.
- **The task is driven once from end to end,** by the keyboard alone, across the surfaces: the
  entry of `by-hand.md` gives the path focus took from the first surface to the last.
- **A judge reads the whole flow.** Its ledger is one, and its earlier ledgers are as many as the
  surfaces it read: its brief names each.
- **The audience reads the task across the surfaces,** from the pictures in the order she would
  meet them.
- **One report, one decision page, one acceptance, one stamp.** A fix names its surface.

## Sized `correction`: one picture beside another

A correction is one wrong thing put right. The handoff is one paragraph, and the session that
conducts applied the change itself. What a correction is, and when it stops being one, is the
conductor's: `skills/design/references/routes.md`, under "A correction".

- **The session that applied it accepts it.** The skill opens by saying you did not build what you
  read. A correction is the one case where that does not hold: the session that conducts made the
  change after his nod, and the same session checks it. That is why it is held to a picture he has
  already seen, and to nothing of your own judgment.
- **No seat is cast,** so nobody reads, and no brief is written. **No script checks a correction:**
  the handoff wrote no `acceptance.json`, and there is no page of a prototype for a check to open.
  Steps 4 to 6 and 8 of the skill are not run.
- **Begin,** as step 1 of the skill says. Copy the template to `<package>/08-acceptance/REPORT.md`,
  with `built-from` as the skill gives it. You read `<package>/07-handoff/HANDOFF.md`: its one
  paragraph, "is → becomes", and the picture it names as done.
- **Photograph the place as built,** at the width she uses, through the driver, into
  `<package>/08-acceptance/shots/`: the picture after, of the build.
- **Compare it with the "becomes" side, by eye:** the one thing reads, or measures, as the paragraph
  says it becomes, and nothing else on the screen moved from the picture he nodded at.
- **One item in the report:** the change, *matches* or *deviates*, with the two pictures that show
  it: the one he nodded at, and the one as built. "The checks" says that no script checks a
  correction, and that it was compared by eye. Every other heading says "Not needed for this
  correction".
- **When it matches, his nod covers it.** A correction costs one nod. Write under
  `## Reviewed by the founder` that the build is what he nodded at, with the words of that nod as
  `HANDOFF.md` has them, show him the picture as built, and stamp with those words, by the `stamp`
  of the skill's gate. Nothing is asked again.
- **When it deviates, it is his to decide,** by the one question of the skill's gate: accepted as
  built, or put right? You put right only what the correction was.
- **The lessons take one line,** unless the correction taught more.

## A fix that comes back

A deviation he wanted fixed went out in `FIXES.md`, and a session built it. The stage was left in
work, unstamped. When the builder says the fix is in:

1. **Read what changed in `<package>/build/`:** the builder brings its three pages up to date with
   the fix, the state map among them.
2. **Drive the states the fix names, and no others,** as steps 3 and 4 of the skill say. Each
   results file of those states is written again, whole, and each picture taken again.
3. **Build the table again,** by the command of step 5, over every file of `results/`.
4. **One re-read, by the seat that found it:** a fresh sub-agent in that seat, handed the new
   pictures and results, and after it has read cold, its ledger of the first read of the build. It
   writes `<package>/08-acceptance/reads/<seat>-reread.md`, beside the first and never over it.
5. **Bring the report up to date:** the item's row, "Fixes sent out", and the count line. Then the
   gate, as the skill gives it.

A fix that did not hold is a deviation again, and his to decide again: you never send the same fix
out twice without asking.
