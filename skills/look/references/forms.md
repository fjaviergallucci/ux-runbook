# Look's other forms: an adjustment, a flow

The skill gives Look as it runs in full, for one surface. Two other forms keep every rule of it and
change only how much is done.

## Routed `short`: an adjustment

Look is what changes, and no more.

- **Only the screens and the states the change touches are redrawn,** by the visual designer the
  cast sheet's "Look" table seats, and those alone are photographed, measured and read, by the
  judges it seats.
- **Every other heading of `LOOK.md` says "Unchanged by this adjustment".**
- **A stage the route skips is left out of `built-from` and of the carry list.**
- **With Structure skipped there is no chosen proposal, and no hand-over names `<chosen>`.** A seat
  takes the structure from what it is handed besides: the states and the rules, and the prototype
  where there is one.
- **With no prototype builder cast at Detail there is no prototype:** no hand-over names its folder
  or its pictures. The visual designer draws from the states, the rules and the strings, on the
  screens of the product as its design system's documents show them.
- **No direction and no fresh pass, unless he asks for one.** The fix round, the re-read and the
  gate are the skill's, unchanged.

## Sized `flow`: surface by surface

A flow is several surfaces that form one task. Look goes surface by surface, in the order Detail
went.

- **What repeats for each surface:** steps 4, 5, 7 and 8 of the skill. One visual designer, a fresh
  sub-agent for each surface, handed the screens of the surfaces already drawn so that the flow
  reads as one; the pictures and the measures; the readers; the one fix round and the one re-read.
- **What every surface writes into, and is one for the flow:** `screens/`, `shots/`, `checks.json`
  and `results.json`, and `measures.md`, whose rows name their screen. In `LOOK.md`, a
  `### <surface>` under each heading that differs from one surface to the next.
- **A reader's file takes the surface's name** after its seat, as
  `<package>/06-look/reads/<seat>-<surface>.md`.
- **What is done once, for the whole flow:** the mode, and a direction when there is none; a fresh
  pass, on the one screen that carries the idea; the variants' axis, which holds on every surface;
  the decision page, with the pictures of each surface among its option's; the gate; the carry
  list; the stamp. He picks or approves the flow, not a surface.
