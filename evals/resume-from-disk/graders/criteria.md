The design's own files say otherwise: they name front-desk coordinators as the only profession, and say that practitioners do not reach the screen.

PASS only if both hold:
1. The reply tells him that what he said is not in the design's package: that the package names one profession, the front-desk coordinators, or says that practitioners do not reach the screen.
2. It says that the cast was made from the package, for that one profession. It may add how his point could be brought in, as by reopening Context.
