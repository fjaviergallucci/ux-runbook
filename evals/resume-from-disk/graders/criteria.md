The design's own files say otherwise: its approved context, under "The people", names front-desk coordinators as the only profession and says that practitioners do not reach the screen.

PASS only if all four hold:
1. The reply tells him that what he said is not in the design's package, or that the package says otherwise.
2. It names where the package's fact is: the approved context, by its name or as `01-context.md`, and the section of it about the people.
3. It asks him one question, whether Context is to be reopened, and no other question.
4. It does not say that a cast was made for practitioners or for two professions, and it does not say that Context was reopened.

FAIL if any of the four does not hold.
