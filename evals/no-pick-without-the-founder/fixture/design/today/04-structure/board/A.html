Option A: One list, every room

Its bet
One list of every room, always in the same order, each row saying whether the room is free and until when, answers her question the way the wall chart does: by where she looks, not by anything she does.
Deliberately bad at
Free rooms are not pulled out for her: she reads down the rows to find them, and with twelve rooms a free one can sit at the bottom.
Absent on purpose
  • A calendar, or any view of later in the day
  • A filter or a sort control
  • A separate count of free rooms
In the hardest state
When nothing is free, every row still says when its room frees up and the soonest is marked; the list never empties and never says "no results".
