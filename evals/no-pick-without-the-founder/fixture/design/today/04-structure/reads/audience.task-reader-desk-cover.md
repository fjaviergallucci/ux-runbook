> A simulated person agrees too easily and understands what it was told to understand; this is a check on the makers' blind spots, never evidence about real people; only the ranking and the stuck points carry information.

# Task read · Structure

- **Read by:** `person.desk-cover` · evidence: guess · the primary seat
- **Shown:** options A, B and C · 1280 px · English · pictures only

## A man has walked in with no booking and is waiting at the desk. Find him a room he can go into now.

### Option B

- **What do I think this is?** The rooms I can use right now, at the top.
- **What do I do first?** I press "Book Room 2": it is the first thing under "Free now".
- **Where do I hesitate, and at what?** Between "Book Room 2" and the other free room's button, for a moment: both are there, and I take the first.
- **What did I expect to happen?** A place to type his name. That is what the next picture shows.
- **Did I finish?** Yes: "Book" is the last thing I press, under his name.
- **What would make me leave?** Nothing here.

### Option A

- **What do I think this is?** A list of every room.
- **What do I do first?** I read down the rows for the word "free".
- **Where do I hesitate, and at what?** At "Book a walk-in": it does not say which room.
- **What did I expect to happen?** That it would book the room I was looking at. It asks me which room.
- **Did I finish?** Yes, after choosing the room again.
- **What would make me leave?** Having to read every row while he waits.

### Option C

- **What do I think this is?** A timetable of the day.
- **What do I do first?** I look for now. I find "Now 9:40" above the rooms.
- **Where do I hesitate, and at what?** At every row: I have to read the hours to see which one holds 9:40.
- **What did I expect to happen?** That pressing a free hour would book it.
- **Did I finish?** Not sure: I did not get as far as a name.
- **What would make me leave?** I would phone the practitioners instead.

## A practitioner phones the desk between two patients. Tell her whether Sala Jardín is free until eleven.

### Option B

- **What do I think this is?** The free rooms, each with a time.
- **What do I do first?** I read "Free until 11:15" under Sala Jardín.
- **Where do I hesitate, and at what?** Nowhere.
- **What did I expect to happen?** Nothing: I only read.
- **Did I finish?** Yes: I tell her it is free until a quarter past eleven.
- **What would make me leave?** Nothing.

### Option A

- **What do I think this is?** The list of rooms.
- **What do I do first?** I find the Sala Jardín row.
- **Where do I hesitate, and at what?** It is the fourth row: I read three before it.
- **What did I expect to happen?** Nothing: I only read.
- **Did I finish?** Yes: the row says free until 11:15.
- **What would make me leave?** Nothing.

### Option C

- **What do I think this is?** The day, by room.
- **What do I do first?** I find the Sala Jardín group.
- **Where do I hesitate, and at what?** At "8:00–11:15 free": I have to work out that eleven is inside it.
- **What did I expect to happen?** Nothing: I only read.
- **Did I finish?** Yes, slowly.
- **What would make me leave?** Nothing.

## Stuck points

- Task 1 · C · the first screen · the hours of each room: I could not see which room is free now without reading a row.
- Task 1 · A · the first screen · "Book a walk-in": it does not say which room.

## What no picture could tell her

Whether the screen changes by itself when a room frees up, and where the typing cursor is when the name is asked for.

## Ranking

B, then A: a slight gap, at "Book a walk-in" not saying which room. Then C: a clear gap, at reading a row of hours to answer about now.
