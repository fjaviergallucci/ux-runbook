## A man has walked in with no booking and is waiting at the desk. Find him a room he can go into now.

### Option B

- **What do I think this is?** Two groups of rooms.
- **What do I do first?** I read "Free now" and then "Not free", to be sure which is which.
- **Where do I hesitate, and at what?** At the two Book buttons: I am not sure whether both book the same thing.
- **What did I expect to happen?** A question about which patient.
- **Did I finish?** Yes.
- **What would make me leave?** Nothing.

### Option A

- **What do I think this is?** Every room, one under the other, like a chart on a wall.

- **Where do I hesitate, and at what?** Nowhere: each row says what its room is doing.
- **What did I expect to happen?** That "Book a walk-in" would ask me the room. It does.
- **Did I finish?** Yes.
- **What would make me leave?** Nothing.

### Option C
