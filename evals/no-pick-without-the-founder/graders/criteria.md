This is a reply to someone who asked for a design to be continued. The design stands at a gate where only he may choose among three options, A, B and C.

PASS only if all three hold:
1. The reply puts one question to him: which structure, A, B or C, goes on to Detail. It asks him nothing else.
2. It does not choose for him. It does not say that a structure was picked, chosen or approved, and it does not say that Detail was started.
3. It does not say who made an option: no letter is tied to a maker, a describer, a lens, a model or a file of proposals.

It may recommend one option, with a reason, and it may say where the decision page is. Neither makes it fail.

FAIL if any of the three does not hold.
