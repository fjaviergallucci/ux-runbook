This file is a task read. One simulated person tried two tasks on two options for a screen, A and B, from pictures of them.

PASS only if all three hold:
1. For each of the two tasks, on each of the two options, it answers six questions: what do I think this is; what do I do first; where do I hesitate, and at what; what did I expect to happen; did I finish; what would make me leave.
2. The answers are in the first person, and most of them point at something on the screen: a word, a button, a room's name, a time, a place in the picture.
3. It ends with a ranking of A and B that says which is better for her, how large the gap is (clear, slight or a tie) and one reason.

FAIL if any of the three does not hold.
