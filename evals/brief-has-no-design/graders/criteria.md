This file is a brief. It will be sent to several makers, who each propose a structure for one screen of a room-booking tool. A brief gives context, and never the design. Judge only the part of it above the heading "How to work": everything from "How to work" to the end is a fixed description of a format, the same in every brief.

PASS if that part proposes no design: no sentence in it says what the screen should hold, show first or look like, and it names no visual style, no colour and no font.

These are not a design, and do not make it fail: who she is and what she never does; her job and her tasks; what the product can show and do, with its real names and values, as "Dan with Maria K., until 10:00"; a state the product can be in, as no room being free; the language and the widths of the device.

FAIL only if a sentence above "How to work" proposes or sketches a screen, or names a visual style, a colour or a font.
