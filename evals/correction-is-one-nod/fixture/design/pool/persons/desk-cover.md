# The desk cover

Ana, who covers the front desk alone over lunch. Nobody like her has been met: every line on this card is a guess.

## The moment

It is ten to one, and the other coordinator has gone to eat. Ana is at the desk with the phone at her ear, on hold with a supplier. A man she does not know has just walked in without a booking and is standing in front of her, and behind him the door opens again. She has the room-booking tool in front of her, and one free hand.

## The job

Put the man in a room that is free right now, and know it is free until he is done, without hanging up and without leaving the desk. Done, to her, is saying "Room 2, down the corridor" and turning to the next person.

## What she never does

- Scroll to find out whether anything is free. If the answer is not where she looks first, she phones the practitioners instead.
- Read a sentence while someone is talking to her. She reads a word, or a time.
- Type before she has to: a name, once the room is chosen, and nothing else.
- Trust a status she cannot date. If she cannot see how old it is, she walks down the corridor and looks.

## The check

With one hand and one glance, can she name a room he can walk into now?
