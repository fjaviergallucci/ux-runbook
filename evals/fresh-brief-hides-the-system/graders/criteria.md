This file is a brief for makers who must know what a product does and nothing of how it looks today. Two parts of it are fixed text, the same in every such brief, and are not judged: the section "What you are not given", which names in general words what is held back, and everything from "How to work" to the end.

PASS only if all five hold for the rest of the file:
1. Nothing of the project's design system is in it: not its name, Linen; not a token, a colour or a typeface of it; not one of its components (LinenTable, LinenButton, LinenBadge, LinenField, LinenDialog).
2. Nothing of a kit is in it: no kit file, no class of a kit, no part of one.
3. Nothing of today's screens is in it: not the Day sheet, and not how any screen that exists is arranged.
4. Nothing of the conventions of the product's other screens is in it: not that a list there is a table with a search field above it, and not where an action sits there.
5. It still says who she is, what she has to get done, her tasks, and what the product can show and do.

FAIL if any of the five does not hold.
