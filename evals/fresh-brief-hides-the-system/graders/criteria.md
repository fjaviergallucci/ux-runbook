This file is a brief for makers who must know what a product does and nothing of how it looks today. Two parts of it are fixed text, the same in every such brief, and are not judged: the section "What you are not given", which names in general words what is held back, and everything from "How to work" to the end.

PASS if the rest of the file holds nothing of how the product looks or is arranged today: nothing of its design system (its name, a token, a colour, a typeface, a component), nothing of a kit, nothing of a screen that exists today, and nothing of how its other screens are laid out or behave.

These do not make it fail: who she is; her job and her tasks; what the product can show and do, with its real names and values; a state the product can be in; the language and the widths of the device.

FAIL only if the judged part names or describes one of those four things.
